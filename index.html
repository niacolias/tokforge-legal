tokForge

tokForge is a personal, self-hosted tool that produces short vertical videos and publishes them to its owner's own YouTube channels.

It is not a public service: it runs on its owner's machine, has no user accounts, and is used only by its owner to manage their own channels.

What it does with YouTube

  • Uploads videos the owner has reviewed and approved to the owner's YouTube channel.
  • Reads the channel's basic identity (name, avatar) to show which channel is connected.
  • Reads statistics of videos it published, so the owner can compare their performance.
